ShiftHue Support

Create your shifts once, tap the days you work, and export your schedule to Calendar.

Get help

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, open a support request on GitHub. Please do not include private schedules, addresses, purchase receipts, or passwords in a public issue.

Quick help

Create a shift: Open Shifts, add a definition, and choose its label, colour, time, reminders, and optional workplace.

Plan a month: Select a shift under the monthly calendar, then tap your workdays. The first month you schedule is your free month.

Export: Open Calendar export, choose a calendar available on your iPhone, review the date range, and confirm. Existing ShiftHue events can be updated; events edited outside ShiftHue are left untouched.

Restore Lifetime Pro: Open Settings → Upgrade → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought Lifetime Pro. If a purchase is pending or the App Store is unavailable, try again later.

Delete data: Remove shifts or assignments in the app, remove exported events in Calendar, or delete ShiftHue from your iPhone to remove its locally stored schedule. Calendar events are separate and must be removed in Calendar.
