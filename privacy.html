Privacy Policy

ShiftHue is a local-first shift planner. This policy describes version 1.0 of the iPhone app and its Home Screen widgets.

Schedules and preferences

Your shift definitions, assignments, workplaces, calendar-event identifiers, and settings are stored on your device and shared with the ShiftHue widget through an Apple App Group. ShiftHue does not provide a developer account, developer-run cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or a backend that receives your schedule.

Calendar

With your permission, ShiftHue reads the calendars available through iOS and creates or updates events in the calendar you choose. The iPhone’s Calendar account provider may sync exported events according to your own account settings, including a Google-backed calendar if configured in iOS. ShiftHue does not sign in to Google or receive your Google credentials. Exported events remain in Calendar if you delete the app; manage them in Calendar.

Workplace search and Maps

If you use workplace search, ShiftHue uses Apple Maps search to return place suggestions and details. Search terms and selected place information may be processed by Apple under its own privacy terms. ShiftHue stores a selected workplace on your device as part of the shift definition; it does not track your live location.

Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant permission, ShiftHue schedules local notifications on your device for the shifts and reminder times you choose. Notification permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is handled by Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment, purchase history, and account information. ShiftHue reads Apple-verified transaction entitlements to unlock unlimited scheduling and support Restore Purchases. ShiftHue does not receive your payment card or sandbox tester password.

Data sharing and retention

ShiftHue does not sell your data or use it for tracking or third-party advertising. The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Locally stored app data remains until you remove it or delete the app, subject to your device backup settings. Calendar events and Apple purchase records are managed separately by their respective services.

Your choices and contact

You can edit or remove shifts and assignments in ShiftHue, revoke Calendar and notification permissions in iOS Settings, and remove the app to delete its local data. For privacy questions, contact ShiftHue support through GitHub; do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
